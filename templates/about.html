About Cineverse

Cineverse is a premium, AI-powered movie discovery platform designed to give you the most accurate and personalized movie recommendations. We believe that finding a great movie should be an immersive experience, not just a list of links.

How It Works (Content-Based Filtering)

Our recommendation engine analyzes the underlying content and metadata of movies to find incredible matches. When you search for a movie, the system:

  • Extracts features like genres and plot overview.
  • Converts the textual data into numerical feature vectors using TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency).
  • Calculates the Cosine Similarity between your chosen movie and thousands of others in our database.
  • Ranks and presents the top 10 most mathematically similar movies to your taste.

Technology Stack
